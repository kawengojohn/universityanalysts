The Rusangu University

The Rusangu University is a private institution located in Lusaka, Monze, and Kitwe and was founded in 2003, committed to provide holistic, quality Christian education that prepares students for productive careers, selfless service to humanity, and eternal life and guided by a vision to be a premier, world-class Christian university of choice, renowned for academic excellence, innovative research, and spiritual transformation.

There are several questions students ask,
some of which have been answered below:

How is first year?

Trick but manageable with strategies and tricks

How do I pack my stuff?

Must have a trunk, and other packages for security.

How do I prepare?

Must have funds to support your needs.

Where can I buy Lab Requirements?

From the local retailers right there in school.

How do I secure my phone and laptop?

Never leave your device, that is, phone or Laptop with anyone you don't know, otherwise your tears will do their work when coming back.

How do I save time?

Avoid irrelevant movements, irrelevant groupings and chats which won't benefit you or anyone. Do something or be involved in situations which build up your life not destroying it.

Should I study all the time?

No, have time to take breaks, have time to pray for yourself and anyone including your enemies, visit your friends and family, have time to talk to them on phone, rest and relax.

How should I study?

People are different and you are yourself. So have time to discover yourself and what works best for you.
