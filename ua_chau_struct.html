The Chalimbana University

The Chalimbana University is a public institution located in Chongwe and was founded in 1939, committed to provide high-quality, inclusive education, research and innovation that produces competent graduates capable of driving national development and community transformation and guided by a vision to be a premier institution recognized for its academic excellence, inclusivity, and capacity to shape ethical, globally competitive, and socially responsible leaders.
