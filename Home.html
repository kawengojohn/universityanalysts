FOCUS ON SUCCESS

One of the fundamendals of Success is the foundation. And no farmer lays a foundation when they see the clouds, therefore prepare early before you arrive at your university. Thank you, stay blessed, secured, purified, protected and guided in Christ by the power of the Holy Spirit.

There are several questions students ask,
some of which have been answered below:

How is first year?

Trick but manageable with strategies and tricks

What rules do I need to know?

Study as early as possible to have a safe and stressless mind. Don't capture and post anyone without their permission

How do I pack my stuff?

Must have a trunk, and other packages for security.

How do I prepare?

Must have funds to support your needs.

Where can I buy Lab Requirements?

From the local retailers right here in school.

How do I secure my phone and laptop?

Never leave your phone or Laptop with anyone you don't know, otherwise your tears are about to start dropping.

How do I save time?

Stop moving anyhow, what is your problem? You came for school.

Should I study all the time?

No, have time to take breaks, have time to pray, visit your friends, family, talk to them on phone, rest and relax.

How should I study?

People are different and you are yourself. So have time to discover what time works well for you. Whether studying before sleeping or after treats you well.
