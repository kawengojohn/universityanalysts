The Chreso University

The Chreso University is a private institution located in Lusaka and was founded in 2010, committed to equip students with outcome-based education, practical skills, and foundational values necessary to excel in their careers, change the African mindset to develop local solutions, and make a positive, transformative impact within their communities and guided by a vision to provide quality, affordable education to the people of Zambia and neighboring coutries by maintaining a transforming learning center equipped with mordern, user-friendly educational facilities that optimize learner potential and social responsibility.
